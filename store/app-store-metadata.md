### Subtitle

### Description
All aboard the Play Train! A cheerful learning game where children aged 4+ trace letters and numbers, count toys, guess animal sounds and collect stickers. Every success puts a new friend on the train.

LETTERS, STEP BY STEP
• Trace every letter of the alphabet, capital and small, as separate stations
• Friendly guidance shows the way: a start dot, arrows, a dotted path and a little caterpillar
• Each letter brings a new friend to meet

NUMBERS AND COUNTING
• Trace the numbers 1 to 20, plus 50, 100 and 1000
• For 1 to 5, count toys and load them onto the train

ANIMALS AND VEHICLES
• Hear an animal sound and pick the right animal
• Which vehicle makes this sound? Can you find the red car?
• Tap the speaker to listen again as often as you like

STICKERS AND CELEBRATIONS
• Every finished station puts a passenger on the train
• A full train means a party with confetti
• New friends move into the sticker album: animals, toys, vehicles and fruit

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• No reading needed: everything is explained by voice
• Big buttons, and every tap gets a reaction
• No losing, no timers and no negative feedback, just another try
• Play in English or Turkish, each with its own alphabet, words and voice

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• No ads, no accounts, no chat and no links out of the app
• No data collected: progress is saved only on the device
• Works fully offline
• Settings and purchases are behind a parent gate

TRY IT FREE
Letters A to F and numbers 1 to 10 are free to play. A single one-time purchase unlocks the full version, with no subscription:
• The whole alphabet, capital and small
• All numbers up to 20, plus 50, 100 and 1000
• Animal sound and vehicle quizzes
• Every new station we add in the future

## Notes for review (App Review Information)

Play Train (Turkish name: Oyun Treni) is an educational game for children aged 4+. Children trace letters and numbers, count toys, answer animal-sound and vehicle questions, and collect stickers. The app is landscape-only and runs on iPhone and iPad (full screen). It is fully localized in English and Turkish.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
There is no sign-up, login or server. No demo account is required. The app works offline.

HOW TO NAVIGATE
1. Home screen: tap the large green Play button.
2. Category menu: Letters, Numbers, Animals, Vehicles.
3. Letters / Numbers open a map of stations. The little train waits at the next suggested station; any open station can be tapped.
4. Tracing: start at the green dot and follow the arrow and dotted line with a finger. Letters with several strokes are traced one stroke at a time. After the shape is finished, a passenger boards the train. For numbers 1–5 the child then drags toys onto the train.
5. Animals and Vehicles open questions directly: a sound plays and the child picks a card. The blue speaker button replays the sound.
6. Every 5 finished stations, a celebration screen shows the full train; the passengers become stickers in the Album (book button, top-left of the home screen).

FREE CONTENT AND IN-APP PURCHASE
Free: letters A–F (English: A B C D E F; Turkish: A B C Ç D E F), each in capital and small form, and numbers 1–10. Everything else is locked: the remaining letters, numbers above 10, and all Animal and Vehicle stations.
There is one Non-Consumable in-app purchase: com.oyuntreni.app.premium ("Full version"). It unlocks all current content and all stations added in future updates. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

HOW TO REACH THE PURCHASE (behind a parent gate)
Option A: Tap any station with a padlock (for example the letter G, the number 11, or the Animals card). A friendly padlock screen appears and a voice says "This station is locked. Ask a grown-up to open it!" Nothing about buying is shown to the child there. Tap "For grown-ups" at the bottom, answer the parent gate, and the purchase panel appears with "Buy" and "Restore purchase".
Option B: On the home screen tap the gear button (top-right), answer the parent gate, and find the "Full version" section with "Buy" and "Restore purchase".

PARENT GATE
A random addition question with a two-digit answer (for example 7 + 6) is shown. Enter the two digits on the keypad. A wrong answer shakes and shows a new question. The question is never read aloud, so a young child who cannot read cannot pass it. The gate protects both the purchase and all settings (language, music, sound effects, reset progress).

RESTORE
"Restore purchase" is available in the purchase panel and in Settings → Full version. After restoring, all stations unlock immediately.

PRIVACY AND SAFETY (Kids Category)
• No data is collected. No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
• No external links, web views, social features or chat for children.
• Progress, stickers and settings are stored only on the device.
• The only network use is StoreKit, for purchase and restore.
• Spoken instructions use the on-device iOS text-to-speech system. The microphone is not used, and no permissions are requested.
• Audio plays even when the silent switch is on, because children who cannot read rely on spoken instructions.

LANGUAGE
The first launch follows the device language (English or Turkish). It can be changed in Settings behind the parent gate.
