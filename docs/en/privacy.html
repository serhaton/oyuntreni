Privacy Policy

Play Train (Oyun Treni) · Effective date: November 8, 2026

In short: Play Train does not collect or share any personal information, and it has no servers of its own. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything your child does in the game stays on your device.

This policy explains how the Play Train app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"), made by Serhat Onal ("we", "us"), handles information. The app is designed for young children, and we built it to collect nothing.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, location, device identifiers, usage statistics or any other personal information. The app has no sign-up or login and no way for a child to enter personal details.

Information stored on your device

To remember your child's progress, the app saves a small amount of information locally on the device:

  • which stations have been finished, the collected stickers and the passengers on the trip train;
  • settings: the chosen language, and whether music and sound effects are on;
  • whether the full version has been unlocked.

This information never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's settings, or by deleting the app. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, which are managed by Apple.

Voice and sound

Spoken instructions are created on the device by the iOS text-to-speech system. The app does not use the microphone and does not record or listen to anything.

In-app purchase

The app offers one optional, one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that the full version was purchased, so it can unlock the content. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases and restores are only available behind a parent gate (a simple question meant for grown-ups).

No ads, analytics or third-party tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services that collect data, no social features, no external links for children and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not use Apple's advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports and basic usage statistics with us through App Store Connect. These reports do not identify you or your child, and you can turn this off at any time in the same place.

Internet connection

The app works offline. It only connects to the internet through Apple's App Store when you buy or restore the full version.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications.

Children's privacy

Play Train is made for children. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. We designed the app with children's privacy laws in mind, such as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the GDPR in the European Union and the Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) in Türkiye. Areas meant for grown-ups (settings and purchases) are protected by a parent gate.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you or your child, we have no data to access, correct, export or delete. All app data stays on your device and is under your control, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change would involve collecting personal information, we will update the app's privacy details on the App Store first.
