All aboard for letters and numbers!

Play Train is a cheerful learning game for children aged 4–5. Kids trace letters and numbers, count toys, guess animal sounds and fill their own sticker album. Every success sends a new friend riding off on the train.

For ages 4–5 English & Turkish No ads No data collected Works offline

What children learn

A

Letters, step by step

Trace every letter of the alphabet, capital and small, following a friendly guide: dots, arrows and a little caterpillar show the way. Each letter brings a new friend to meet.

3

Numbers and counting

Trace the numbers 1 to 20, plus 50, 100 and 1000. For 1 to 5, children also count toys and load them onto the train.

?

Animal sounds

Listen to a real animal sound and pick the right animal. Tap the speaker to hear it again as many times as you like.

!

Vehicles and colors

Which vehicle makes this sound? Can you find the red car? Playful questions about vehicles, sounds and colors.

★

Stickers and celebrations

Every finished station puts a passenger on the train. A full train means a party with confetti, and the new friends move into the sticker album.

♥

No reading needed

Friendly voice guidance, big buttons and gentle feedback. There is no losing and nothing negative, just another try.
