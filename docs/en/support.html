Playing

How does my child play?

Tap the big green play button and choose a category: Letters, Numbers, Animals or Vehicles. Every station is open, so your child can start anywhere. The little train waits at the next suggested station.

Everything is explained by voice, so no reading is needed. A wrong answer only means another try. There is no losing and no time limit.

How does letter and number tracing work?

Start at the green dot and follow the arrow and the dotted line with a finger. Letters with several strokes are traced one stroke at a time. When the shape is finished, its passenger hops onto the train.

If a stroke doesn't count, start it again from its beginning and stay close to the line.

What do the check marks and stickers mean?

A check mark shows a finished station. Each finished station also puts a passenger on the train. When the train is full there is a celebration, and the passengers become stickers in the album (the book button on the home screen).

Which ages is Play Train for?

Play Train is designed for children aged 4–5. Younger children enjoy it with a grown-up nearby, and older children can use it to practise.

Settings

How do I open the settings?

Tap the gear button in the top-right corner of the home screen and answer the small math question. This parent gate keeps settings and purchases away from little fingers.

How do I change the language?

Open the settings and choose English or Türkçe. The alphabet, the words and the voice all change, and progress is kept separately for each language.

How do I turn music or sounds off?

In the settings you can turn off the background music and the sound effects separately. Spoken instructions and the sounds that are part of a question, such as the animal sounds, keep playing so the game stays playable.

I can't hear the voice or any sound.

Check the device volume first. If it still doesn't work, check that the spoken voice for the chosen language is installed on the device (Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices), then close and reopen Play Train.

How do I start over?

In the settings, tap "Reset progress" and confirm. This removes all check marks, stickers and the trip train. It cannot be undone.

Full version and purchases

What is free and what is in the full version?

Free: letters A to F (capital and small) and numbers 1 to 10. The full version unlocks the whole alphabet, all numbers (up to 20, plus 50, 100 and 1000) and the animal and vehicle quizzes.

Every new station we add in the future is included too, at no extra cost. It is a single one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I buy the full version?

Tap a station with a padlock and then "For grown-ups", or open the settings. After the parent gate, tap the buy button and confirm with your Apple Account. Payment is handled by Apple.

I bought the full version, but stations are still locked (new device or reinstall).

Open the settings, go to "Full version" and tap "Restore purchase". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. You need an internet connection for this step.

The purchase says it is waiting for approval.

If Ask to Buy is turned on for your family, the purchase waits until the family organizer approves it. Once approved, the full version unlocks automatically, or you can tap "Restore purchase".

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy and safety

Does Play Train collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics services or tracking. Progress and settings are stored only on your device. Read the full privacy policy.

Does it need the internet?

No, Play Train works fully offline. The internet is only needed to buy or restore the full version through the App Store.
